📊 Exploratory Data Analysis – Brazilian E-Commerce (Olist)
--
📌 Project Overview
---

This project performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a real Brazilian e-commerce dataset with the goal of uncovering business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, logistics efficiency, and product categories.

The analysis focuses on transforming raw transactional data into actionable insights that could support strategic and operational decision-making.

----

🎯 Business Objectives:
---

Understand sales and revenue behavior over time

Identify top-performing product categories

Analyze geographic distribution of orders and revenue

Evaluate logistics performance and its impact on customer satisfaction

Highlight patterns, anomalies, and opportunities for improvement

🗂 Dataset:
---

Source: Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (Olist)

The dataset contains multiple relational tables representing a real e-commerce operation, including:

Orders

Order items

Products

Customers

Payments

Customer reviews

Sellers

🛠 Tools & Technologies:
---

Python 3

Pandas

NumPy

Matplotlib

Seaborn

Jupyter Notebook

----

🔍 Analysis Workflow:
--

1.Data Loading & Inspection

Schema validation

Missing values and duplicates analysis

2.Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering

Date parsing

Order value calculation

Delivery delay indicators

3.Temporal Analysis

Monthly revenue trends

Order volume evolution

Average ticket behavior

4.Category Analysis

Revenue concentration

Top categories by volume and value

5.Geographic Analysis

Orders and revenue by state

Regional ticket comparison

6.Logistics & Customer Experience

Delivery delays

Impact of late deliveries on review scores

----

📈 Key Insights:
--

Revenue shows clear temporal patterns and seasonality

A small subset of categories accounts for a large portion of total revenue

Certain regions generate high order volume but lower average ticket

Late deliveries are strongly associated with lower review scores, indicating a direct impact on customer satisfaction
